Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

If you sign up for free printables, we collect your email address and the age group you choose. If you sponsor a classroom set, we also collect the name and message you ask us to print, and the payment provider (such as Stripe or PayPal) handles your payment details. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

We use your email to send the printables you asked for, news about new books, and photos of classroom deliveries you sponsored. We don’t sell or share your information. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Children

This site is for parents, teachers and other adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we’ll delete it.

School photos

We only share photos from school deliveries with the school’s permission, and we avoid showing children’s faces unless their parents have agreed in writing.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the email address in the footer of our emails.
