Bright Trails Gives

Every book helps a child in a Philippine public school

Many families here can’t afford books or school supplies. So we print books locally and give them to public school classes, each with crayons and a pencil.

Buy a book, give a book

Part of every Bright Trails sale pays for a school kit: a coloring or story book, a box of crayons and a pencil, delivered to a child in a public school.

A small family publisher

Bright Trails Publishing is a family-run imprint based in the Philippines. We make books we want for our own home: calm stories at bedtime, coloring pages with real facts, and tracing books that help busy minds slow down.

Our children’s books are written by Mary Jane Baga Martimbor.
